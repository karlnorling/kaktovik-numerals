Kaktovik Numerals

Draw any number in the base-20 digits Iñupiaq students in Kaktovik, Alaska, invented in 1994: fives on top, units below. SVG with zero dependencies, plus PNG, JPEG and WebP.

Try any number

The playground draws whatever you type, breaks it into base-20 digits, and lets you download SVG, PNG, JPEG or WebP.

Fractions and negatives

Fractions continue after a dot in base 20, exactly, and negatives get a minus sign. See how.

Zero dependencies

@kaktovik-numerals/core turns a number into an SVG string in any JavaScript runtime.

Images and CLI

@kaktovik-numerals/raster renders PNG, JPEG and WebP with sharp, and @kaktovik-numerals/cli does it from the terminal.
